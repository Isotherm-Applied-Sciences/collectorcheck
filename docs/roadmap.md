# Isotherm Applied Sciences — Unified Product Roadmap

---

## Purpose

This roadmap unifies the development trajectories of CollectorCheck, HydroPass, Databus, and the Isotherm Identity Substrate. It provides a deterministic, continuity-first plan for product evolution across the ecosystem.

---

## Roadmap Structure

- **CollectorCheck Roadmap**
- **HydroPass Roadmap**
- **Databus Roadmap**
- **Identity Substrate Roadmap**
- **Cross-Product Milestones**
- **Release Sequencing**
- **Versioning Strategy**

---

## Why a Unified Roadmap Matters

A unified roadmap ensures that identity, continuity, and provenance evolve together across all Isotherm products. It prevents fragmentation, maintains deterministic behavior, and supports long-term trust-traffic.

## Cross-Product Milestones

1. **Unified Identity Attribute Schema** — Shared identity attributes across HydroPass, Databus, and CollectorCheck.
2. **Unified Lineage Model** — Shared identity, activity, credential, and verification lineage.
3. **Unified Continuity Model** — Shared continuity rules across all products.
4. **Unified Provenance Surface** — Shared provenance metadata across the ecosystem.
5. **Unified Identity Substrate Specification** — Single substrate architecture governing all identity flows.

1. **CollectorCheck v1.0** — Deterministic verification gateway.
2. **HydroPass v1.0** — Dignity-first identity credential.

---

## Versioning Strategy

- **Major Versions** — Introduce new substrate-level capabilities or cross-product integration.
- **Minor Versions** — Add new API endpoints, lineage types, or continuity rules.
- **Patch Versions** — Fix deterministic behavior, continuity drift, or provenance inconsistencies.
