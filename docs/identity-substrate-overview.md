# Isotherm Identity Substrate — Company-Wide Architecture Overview

---

## Purpose

The Isotherm Identity Substrate unifies CollectorCheck, HydroPass, and Databus into a single deterministic architecture for continuity, provenance, and trust. It provides the structural foundation for identity transport, verification flows, and lineage across ecosystems.

## Why This Architecture Matters

The identity substrate ensures that identity, activity, and provenance remain stable, inspectable, and trustworthy across platforms, products, and verification surfaces.

---

## System Architecture Overview

The Isotherm Identity Substrate is composed of three coordinated systems:

1. **HydroPass — Identity Credential Layer** — Provides stable, dignity-first identity attributes with minimal exposure.
2. **CollectorCheck — Verification Gateway Layer** — Produces deterministic verification results using identity, activity, and provenance.
3. **Databus — Substrate Transport Layer** — Carries identity, continuity, and provenance across platforms and verification flows.

These systems form a unified identity substrate that supports deterministic trust-traffic.

---

## Cross-Product Identity Flow

Identity flows through the substrate in the following order:

1. **HydroPass** provides stable identity attributes.
2. **Databus** transports identity and continuity metadata.
3. **CollectorCheck** consumes identity and provenance to produce deterministic verification results.

This flow ensures:
- stable identity
- deterministic verification
- continuity-safe provenance
- minimal exposure
