# Isotherm Identity Substrate — Company-Wide Architecture Overview

---

## Purpose

The Isotherm Identity Substrate unifies CollectorCheck, HydroPass, and Databus into a single deterministic architecture for continuity, provenance, and trust. It provides the structural foundation for identity transport, verification flows, and lineage across ecosystems.

---

## Core Principles

- **Deterministic identity transport**
- **Continuity-first design**
- **Unified provenance model**
- **Minimal exposure**
- **Cross-product stability**
- **Inspectable verification flows**

---

## Products in the Substrate

- **HydroPass** — dignity-first identity credential
- **CollectorCheck** — deterministic verification gateway
- **Databus** — universal identity substrate

---

## Why This Architecture Matters

The identity substrate ensures that identity, activity, and provenance remain stable, inspectable, and trustworthy across platforms, products, and verification surfaces.
